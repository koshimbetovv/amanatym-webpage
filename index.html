Questions, answered

A few things worth knowing.

Amanatym helps people discover and contact each other around a shared route.

Amanatym is a platform where travelers can post upcoming trips and senders can find people already going to their destination.

Travelers add an expected fee when posting a trip. Both sides can discuss the final details directly in messages.

Listings can range from documents to small or medium items, depending on the traveler's available capacity and applicable rules.

Travelers should inspect every item and ensure it complies with airline, customs, and destination-country requirements before accepting it.
